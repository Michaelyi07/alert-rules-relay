## Security & scope
- Webhook secrets: env/KMS only. Live-drill URL lives in a gitignored local file, is read into the child process env, and never appears in code, evidence, or logs (the drill log shows channel names only).
- Serializer whitelist: only alertname/env/severity/instance + dashboard_url/runbook_url/summary survive; other labels/annotations (including PII-like) are dropped before rendering.
- env=dev -> noop (no notification); unknown env/severity -> fallback WITH warning, never silently dropped.
- In: rule files, routing config, relay service + tests, threshold table, live group screenshots. Out: SMS/phone escalation, full runbook text, third-party costs, real customer data.
